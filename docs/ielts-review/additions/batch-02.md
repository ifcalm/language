## 阅读 02 · 因果与逻辑关系(9)

| 词 | 释义 | 例句 | 译文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| albeit | conj. 尽管,虽然 | The economy continued to grow, **albeit** more slowly than before. | 经济继续增长,尽管比以前慢。 |
| via | prep. 经由;通过 | Most of the goods reach the island **via** the port in the north. | 大部分货物经由北部的港口运抵该岛。 |
| whilst | conj. 然而,而;当…的时候 | **Whilst** sales of books fell, sales of magazines remained steady. | 图书销量下降,而杂志销量保持平稳。 |
| versus | prep. 与…相对;对阵 | The debate about nature **versus** nurture has continued for over a century. | 关于先天与后天的争论已经持续了一个多世纪。 |
| alongside | prep. 与…一起;在…旁边 | Volunteers worked **alongside** scientists to count the birds on the island. | 志愿者与科学家一起清点岛上的鸟类。 |
| amid | prep. 在…之中 | The factory closed **amid** growing concern about pollution in the river. | 在人们对河流污染日益担忧的情况下,工厂关闭了。 |
| beforehand | adv. 事先,预先 | Visitors are advised to book their tickets at least a week **beforehand**. | 建议游客至少提前一周订票。 |
| however | adv. 然而;无论如何 | The method is cheap; **however**, it does not work in cold weather. | 这种方法成本低,然而在寒冷天气里不起作用。 |
| meanwhile | adv. 与此同时 | **Meanwhile**, the cost of housing in the capital has continued to rise. | 与此同时,首都的住房成本持续上涨。 |

## 写作 02 · 常用短语与固定搭配(5)

| 词 | 释义 | 例句 | 译文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| for example | phr. 例如,比如 | Some cities, **for example** Amsterdam, have more bicycles than cars. | 有些城市,例如阿姆斯特丹,自行车比汽车还多。 |
| in order to | phr. 为了 | Many people move to cities **in order to** find better-paid work. | 许多人为了找到收入更高的工作而搬到城市。 |
| even though | phr. 尽管,即使 | **Even though** the tax was unpopular, it reduced traffic in the centre. | 尽管这项税不受欢迎,但它减少了市中心的交通流量。 |
| so that | phr. 以便,为了 | Schools should open later **so that** teenagers can get enough sleep. | 学校应该晚些上课,以便青少年能有充足的睡眠。 |
| as long as | phr. 只要;长达 | Tourism can benefit a region **as long as** it is carefully managed. | 只要管理得当,旅游业可以让一个地区受益。 |
