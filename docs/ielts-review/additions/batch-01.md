# 新增词条第 1 批:学术形容词(40 个,待确认)

归入:阅读 02 · 学术形容词与副词。尚未写入 D1;音标和音频在释义、例句确认后再做。

| 词 | 释义 | 例句 | 译文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| initial | adj. 最初的 n. 首字母 | The **initial** results of the trial were more positive than researchers had expected. | 试验的初步结果比研究人员预期的更乐观。 |
| subsequent | adj. 随后的 | **Subsequent** studies have failed to confirm the findings of the original experiment. | 后续研究未能证实最初实验的结论。 |
| ultimate | adj. 最终的;终极的 | The **ultimate** aim of the project is to reduce poverty in rural areas. | 该项目的最终目标是减少农村地区的贫困。 |
| inevitable | adj. 不可避免的 | Some loss of wildlife is **inevitable** when a new road is built. | 修建新公路时,野生动物的部分损失不可避免。 |
| relevant | adj. 相关的;切题的 | Applicants should have at least two years of **relevant** work experience. | 申请人应至少有两年相关工作经验。 |
| stable | adj. 稳定的 | Children need a **stable** home life in order to do well at school. | 孩子需要稳定的家庭生活才能在学校表现良好。 |
| identical | adj. 完全相同的 | The two groups were given **identical** tasks under different conditions. | 两组人在不同条件下接受了完全相同的任务。 |
| arbitrary | adj. 武断的,任意的 | The age limit of eighteen seems rather **arbitrary** to many young people. | 在许多年轻人看来,十八岁这个年龄限制相当武断。 |
| mutual | adj. 相互的;共同的 | A good working relationship depends on **mutual** respect and trust. | 良好的工作关系取决于相互尊重和信任。 |
| comprehensive | adj. 全面的,综合的 | The report provides a **comprehensive** review of research on climate change. | 该报告对气候变化研究作了全面综述。 |
| alternative | n. 替代方案 adj. 可替代的 | Cycling is a cheap and healthy **alternative** to driving in cities. | 在城市里,骑自行车是替代开车的一种便宜又健康的选择。 |
| neutral | adj. 中立的;中性的 | Journalists are expected to remain **neutral** when reporting on political issues. | 记者在报道政治问题时应保持中立。 |
| dynamic | adj. 充满活力的;动态的 | The city has a young population and a **dynamic** economy. | 这座城市人口年轻,经济充满活力。 |
| visual | adj. 视觉的 | Young children often respond better to **visual** information than to written text. | 幼儿对视觉信息的反应往往比对文字更好。 |
| internal | adj. 内部的 | The company carried out an **internal** review of its safety procedures. | 公司对其安全规程进行了内部审查。 |
| corporate | adj. 公司的,企业的 | Large firms spend millions each year on improving their **corporate** image. | 大公司每年花费数百万来改善企业形象。 |
| liberal | adj. 开明的;自由主义的 | Attitudes towards marriage have become more **liberal** over the last fifty years. | 过去五十年里,人们对婚姻的态度变得更加开明。 |
| sole | adj. 唯一的 | Fishing was once the **sole** source of income for the village. | 捕鱼曾是这个村庄唯一的收入来源。 |
| minimum | adj. 最低的,最小的 n. 最低限度 | Workers must be paid at least the **minimum** wage set by the government. | 工人的工资不得低于政府规定的最低工资。 |
| minor | adj. 较小的,次要的 n. 未成年人 | Only a few **minor** changes were made to the original design. | 原设计只作了几处小改动。 |
| prior | adj. 先前的;优先的 | No **prior** knowledge of the subject is required for this course. | 这门课程不要求事先具备该学科的知识。 |
| definite | adj. 明确的,确定的 | The council has not yet set a **definite** date for the opening. | 市政委员会尚未确定开放的确切日期。 |
| conscious | adj. 意识到的;有意识的 | Consumers are becoming more **conscious** of the environmental cost of their choices. | 消费者越来越意识到自己的选择所带来的环境代价。 |
| numerous | adj. 许多的 | **Numerous** studies have shown a link between exercise and mental health. | 大量研究表明,锻炼与心理健康之间存在联系。 |
| scarce | adj. 稀缺的 | Fresh water is becoming **scarce** in many parts of the world. | 淡水在世界许多地方正变得稀缺。 |
| ample | adj. 充裕的,绰绰有余的 | There is **ample** evidence that smoking damages the lungs. | 有充分的证据表明吸烟损害肺部。 |
| prone | adj. 易于…的;有…倾向的 | Low-lying coastal areas are particularly **prone** to flooding. | 地势低洼的沿海地区特别容易发生洪水。 |
| dense | adj. 茂密的,稠密的;浓的 | The valley is covered in **dense** forest that few people have explored. | 山谷覆盖着茂密的森林,很少有人探索过。 |
| competent | adj. 能胜任的,称职的 | A **competent** manager knows how to get the best out of a team. | 称职的管理者懂得如何让团队发挥出最佳水平。 |
| inferior | adj. 较差的,次等的 | Cheaper products are not always **inferior** in quality to expensive ones. | 便宜的产品在质量上并不总是比昂贵的差。 |
| quantitative | adj. 定量的,数量的 | The study combined **quantitative** data with interviews of local residents. | 该研究把定量数据与对当地居民的访谈结合起来。 |
| collective | adj. 集体的,共同的 | Protecting the oceans is the **collective** responsibility of all nations. | 保护海洋是所有国家的共同责任。 |
| partial | adj. 部分的;偏袒的 | The new law has been only a **partial** success so far. | 到目前为止,这项新法律只取得了部分成功。 |
| massive | adj. 巨大的,大规模的 | The earthquake caused **massive** damage to roads and bridges in the region. | 地震对该地区的道路和桥梁造成了巨大破坏。 |
| simultaneous | adj. 同时的,同时发生的 | The two discoveries were almost **simultaneous**, although the scientists had never met. | 这两项发现几乎同时出现,尽管两位科学家从未见过面。 |
| integral | adj. 不可或缺的;完整的 | Music is an **integral** part of the culture of the island. | 音乐是该岛文化中不可或缺的一部分。 |
| concurrent | adj. 并存的,同时进行的 | The rise in car ownership was **concurrent** with a decline in bus use. | 汽车保有量上升的同时,公共汽车的使用在下降。 |
| ignorant | adj. 不了解的;无知的 | Many people are still **ignorant** of the health risks of too little sleep. | 许多人仍然不了解睡眠过少带来的健康风险。 |
| informal | adj. 非正式的 | Much of the learning in early childhood takes place in **informal** settings. | 幼儿期的许多学习是在非正式的环境中进行的。 |
| hostile | adj. 敌对的,怀有敌意的;恶劣的 | Local people were at first **hostile** to the idea of a new airport. | 当地人起初对建新机场的想法怀有敌意。 |
