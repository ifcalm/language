| 例句 | 释义 | 修改前 | 修改后 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| proof-1 | n. 证据,证明 | So far, scientists have found no **proof** of life on other planets.<br>到目前为止,科学家还没有找到其他星球存在生命的证明。 | So far, scientists have found no **proof** of life on other planets.<br>到目前为止,科学家还没有找到其他星球存在生命的证据。 | 欠佳:译文“找到……的证明”不自然 |
| suggest-1 | v. 表明;建议 | Recent findings **suggest** that bees can recognise individual human faces.<br>最新发现表明,蜜蜂能够识别人类的面孔。 | Recent findings **suggest** that bees can recognise individual human faces.<br>最新发现表明,蜜蜂能够辨认不同的人脸。 | 欠佳:译文漏了 individual,原句是能分辨不同的人 |
| merit-1 | n. 优点 | Each of these approaches has its own **merit**, depending on the situation.<br>根据情况不同,这些方法各有优点。 | Each of these approaches has its own **merits**, depending on the situation.<br>根据情况不同,这些方法各有优点。 | 欠佳:has its own merit 通常用复数 merits |
| necessity-1 | n. 必要性;必需品 | The drought made careful use of water an absolute **necessity** for farmers.<br>干旱使节约用水成为农民的绝对必要之事。 | The drought made careful use of water an absolute **necessity** for farmers.<br>干旱让节约用水成了农民非做不可的事。 | 欠佳:译文“绝对必要之事”生硬 |
| manipulation-1 | n. 操纵 | The photographs showed clear signs of **manipulation** by computer.<br>这些照片有明显的计算机操纵痕迹。 | The photographs showed clear signs of **manipulation** by computer.<br>这些照片有明显经过电脑处理的痕迹。 | 欠佳:译文“计算机操纵痕迹”不是中文说法 |
| facet-1 | n. 方面;刻面 | Language is only one **facet** of a people's cultural identity.<br>语言只是一个民族文化身份的一个方面。 | Language is only one **facet** of a people's cultural identity.<br>语言只是民族文化身份的一个方面。 | 欠佳:译文“一个民族……的一个方面”重复 |
| tendency-1 | n. 倾向,趋势 | People have a natural **tendency** to remember bad news better than good news.<br>人们天生倾向于比好消息更容易记住坏消息。 | People have a natural **tendency** to remember bad news better than good news.<br>人们天生有一种倾向:坏消息比好消息记得更牢。 | 欠佳:译文“倾向于比好消息更容易记住坏消息”不通顺 |
| sustainable-1 | adj. 可持续的 | Farmers are being encouraged to adopt more **sustainable** methods of fishing.<br>人们鼓励渔民采用更可持续的捕鱼方法。 | Fishermen are being encouraged to adopt more **sustainable** fishing methods.<br>人们正鼓励渔民采用更可持续的捕鱼方法。 | 错误:英文主语是 farmers(农民),却说 methods of fishing,译文又写成渔民,前后不一致 |
| supplementary-1 | adj. 补充的 | **Supplementary** classes were offered to children who had fallen behind.<br>学校为落后的孩子提供了补充课程。 | **Supplementary** classes were offered to children who had fallen behind.<br>学校为学习跟不上的孩子开设了补充课程。 | 欠佳:译文“落后的孩子”有歧义,原句指学习落后 |
| address-1 | v. 处理,应对;发表演说 n. 地址 | The report fails to **address** the causes of poverty in rural areas.<br>这份报告未能处理农村贫困的根源。 | The report fails to **address** the causes of poverty in rural areas.<br>这份报告没有探讨农村贫困的根源。 | 欠佳:报告“处理”根源说不通,这里 address 指探讨、正视 |
| secure-2 | v. 获得;确保 adj. 安全的,稳定的 | Most people want a **secure** job that offers a regular income.<br>大多数人都想要一份收入稳定的安稳工作。 | Most people want a **secure** job that offers a regular income.<br>大多数人都想要一份有固定收入的稳定工作。 | 欠佳:译文“收入稳定的安稳工作”重复 |
| clarify-1 | v. 澄清 | Further tests are needed to **clarify** the cause of the illness.<br>需要进一步检查来澄清病因。 | Further tests are needed to **clarify** the cause of the illness.<br>需要进一步检查才能查明病因。 | 欠佳:“澄清病因”搭配不当,中文说“查明” |
| distort-1 | v. 歪曲 | The curved surface of the Earth tends to **distort** flat maps.<br>地球的曲面往往会使平面地图变形。 | Flat maps inevitably **distort** the curved surface of the Earth.<br>平面地图难免会使地球的曲面变形。 | 欠佳:原句说地球曲面使平面地图变形,逻辑颠倒;是把曲面画成平面时产生变形 |
| confine-1 | v. 限制;禁闭 | The disease was **confined** to a small area in the south of the country.<br>这种疾病被限制在该国南部的一小片地区。 | The disease was **confined** to a small area in the south of the country.<br>这种疾病仅局限在该国南部的一小片地区。 | 欠佳:was confined to 指局限于,译成“被限制在”像是人为控制 |
| bind-1 | v. 约束;捆绑 | The treaty will **bind** all member countries to reducing their carbon emissions.<br>这项条约将约束所有成员国减少碳排放。 | The treaty will **bind** all member countries to reduce their carbon emissions.<br>这项条约将约束所有成员国减少碳排放。 | 欠佳:bind sb to doing 不常见,通常说 bind sb to do |
| conform-1 | v. 遵守;符合 | Children often feel pressure to **conform** to the behaviour of their friends.<br>孩子们常常感到要与朋友的行为保持一致的压力。 | Children often feel pressure to **conform** to the behaviour of their friends.<br>孩子们常常感到压力,觉得自己的行为必须和朋友一样。 | 欠佳:译文“感到要与……保持一致的压力”套英文结构 |
| discard-1 | v. 丢弃 | Most of the fish caught by the boats are **discarded** as unwanted.<br>船只捕捞的大部分鱼都被当作不需要的东西丢弃了。 | Many of the fish caught by the boats are **discarded** because nobody wants them.<br>船只捕到的鱼有很多因为没人要而被丢弃。 | 欠佳:说捕获的鱼“大部分”被丢弃言过其实 |
| necessitate-1 | v. 使成为必要 | The growth of the city will **necessitate** a new water supply.<br>城市的发展使得新的供水系统成为必要。 | The growth of the city will **necessitate** a new water supply.<br>城市不断发展,必须建设新的供水系统。 | 欠佳:译文“使得……成为必要”生硬 |
| reverse-2 | v. 逆转 adj. 相反的 | The second study produced exactly the **reverse** result.<br>第二项研究得出了完全相反的结果。 | The events are described in **reverse** order, beginning with the most recent.<br>这些事件按相反的顺序叙述,从最近发生的讲起。 | 欠佳:exactly the reverse result 不是常见搭配,形容词 reverse 常用于 in reverse order |
