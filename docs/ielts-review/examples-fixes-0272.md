| adventurous-1 | adj. 爱冒险的 | I'm not very **adventurous**, so I usually order the same food every time.<br>我不太爱冒险,所以每次都点同样的食物。 | I'm not very **adventurous**, so I usually order the same dish when I eat out.<br>我不太爱冒险,所以在外面吃饭通常都点同一道菜。 | 欠佳:usually 和 every time 语义重复 |
| content-1 | adj. 满足的 n. 内容 | I'm pretty **content** with my life at the moment, to be honest.<br>说实话,我对现在的生活挺满足的。 | I'm **content** with a simple life and don't need expensive things.<br>我满足于简单的生活,不需要昂贵的东西。 | 欠佳:和相邻的 satisfied-1 几乎同一句话(I'm pretty … with my life at the moment) |
| garment-1 | n. 服装 | That jacket is my favourite **garment**, and I've had it since I was eighteen.<br>那件夹克是我最喜欢的衣服,我十八岁起就有了。 | Delicate **garments** like this silk shirt should always be washed by hand.<br>像这件真丝衬衫这样娇贵的衣服一定要手洗。 | 欠佳:my favourite garment 在口语里很别扭,garment 多用于正式或洗涤说明的语境 |
